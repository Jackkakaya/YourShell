# Community code of conduct

YourShell contributors are expected to keep discussion technical, respectful,
and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent while evaluating evidence carefully.
- Respect different experience levels and communication styles.
- Keep reviews focused on the contribution, not the contributor.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption, and sustained personal attacks are not tolerated in project
spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.
